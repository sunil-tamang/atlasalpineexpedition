01Your privacy

Thank you for visiting Atlas Alpine Expedition


Your privacy is important to Atlas Alpine Expedition and we respects your privacy and recognizes the need to protect the personal information (the data that can be used to identify or contact a person). We assure you that we follow appropriate standards when it comes to protecting your privacy on our website.

02What personal info we collect

  • Name of the User
  • Identification Proof (Passport, Aadhar, Voter ID etc.)
  • Correspondance and Permanent Address
  • Contact Info such as mobile number or Email ID

03How we use your personal info

  • We may use your personal information, including date of birth, to verify identity, and to assist us with identification of users.
  • We may use your personal information to send important notices, such as a change of plans for the trip affected due to some natural, political event, or changes to our terms and conditions, and policies.
  • We may also use your personal information for internal purposes such as data analysis, and research to improve our products, services, and customer communications.
