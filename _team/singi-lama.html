--- layout: guide-profile title: Singi Lama - Managing Director description: Meet Singi Lama, Managing Director of Atlas Alpine Expedition. permalink: /team/singi-lama/ excerpt: "Meet the person behind the planning, partnerships, and daily work at Atlas Alpine Expedition." page_class: page-guide-profile custom_css: guide-profile.css profile_name: Singi Lama profile_role: Managing Director profile_photo: /assets/css/images/team/04.jpeg --- Singi Lama oversees the daily running of Atlas Alpine Expedition, working closely with our guides, local partners, and guests to bring each journey together. His work starts well before a team reaches the trail. From reviewing itineraries and coordinating logistics to answering guests’ questions, Singi helps make sure everyone knows what to expect. He believes a good expedition depends on honest communication, careful preparation, and people who look after one another. He stays involved throughout the planning process and supports the field team as plans develop. Away from the office, Singi enjoys walking, visiting mountain communities, and spending time with family. ## At Atlas Alpine - Expedition planning and operations - Guest communication and trip coordination - Team support and local partnerships - Company development and direction
